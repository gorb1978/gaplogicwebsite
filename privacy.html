1. Data Collection and Storage (Local-First Architecture)

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal, financial, or health data. Our applications are designed with a "local-only" architecture. All schedules, ledgers, notes, logs, and profiles you create are stored on your personal device. Our apps have no ability to send that data to us or to anyone else. Android's own system backup may include app data in your personal Google Drive backup — see "Android system backup" below. We do not operate cloud servers to process your private data, we do not require account creation, and we have absolutely no access to the information you enter into our apps.

Example: GapRegister's On-Device-Only Features

GapRegister's optional PIN app lock, full local backup & restore, and receipt photo attachments all follow this same rule. Every transaction, category and setting lives in a single database on this device, and GapRegister has no way to send it anywhere. If you have Android's own backup switched on, the system may include that database in your personal Google Drive backup — that is Android doing it, not us, it is encrypted with your device PIN, and you can turn it off in Android Settings. GapRegister's own optional app-lock PIN is separate from your device PIN: it is stored on your device as a salted hash, and we specifically exclude app settings from Android's backup, so that PIN cannot leave your phone.

Android system backup

Android includes a built-in backup service that can copy an app's data into your own Google Drive. It is part of the operating system rather than part of our apps, and you control it.

Anything backed up goes to your own Google Drive account

It is not sent to GapLogic, and we have no access to it.

Android encrypts these backups

Using your device PIN, pattern or password.

You can turn it off at any time

In Android's Settings, usually under System, then Backup.

We deliberately exclude app settings

Including your GapRegister PIN, from this backup, so credentials never travel between devices.

We deliberately allow your records to be included

So you do not lose your history when you replace your phone.

Our apps still cannot transmit your data themselves. GapRegister, for example, declares no internet permission at all, which you can verify for yourself in Android's App info screen.
